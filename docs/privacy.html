Privacy policy: Knowtorious uploader

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the app is

Knowtorious uploader is a private tool that the owner of the YouTube channel @KnowtoriousDaily runs on his own computer to upload that channel's videos. It has one user: the channel owner. It is not distributed or offered to the public.

What data it uses

With the owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's channel and set their title, description, tags, visibility and thumbnail;
  • read basic channel information (channel name, subscriber and video counts) to confirm it is connected to the right channel;
  • read YouTube's public list of popular videos, as research for video ideas.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not send data anywhere except to Google's YouTube API.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also disconnects the app.

Contact

Questions: through the @KnowtoriousDaily channel.
